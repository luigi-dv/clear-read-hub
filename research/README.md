# Research workspace

Working files for the ADHD readability study. Nothing here ships with the service.

- `research_question.md`: the one-page research question, hypotheses, outcomes and
  design. Edit freely; it becomes the preregistration.
- `evidence_matrix.csv`: one row per study. Pre-filled from the literature review in
  `../reports/ADHD reading accessibility literature.md`. Fields marked `?` were not
  retrievable without the full text. Fill them while reading, set `read_status` to
  `read`, and record disagreements with the pre-filled summary in `my_notes`.
  Check the `verification` column before citing: anything other than "DOI verified"
  must be confirmed against the publisher page.
- `../research_notes/`: the raw notes behind the report, with source links.

Reading order: rows M1 to M6 (mechanisms), I1 to I6 (interventions), H1 to H4
(HCI framing and method templates), N1 (a clean null result).
